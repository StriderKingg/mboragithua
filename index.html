Case 01 · Remittance fintech · NALA · 2024–2026

EDD at volume across remittance corridors

The problem. High-risk customers and counterparties sending money across several corridors, each carrying sanctions, fraud and reputational exposure, with a caseload that never paused.

What I did. Applied a FATF-aligned, risk-based approach to 100+ cases a month: screening, open-source verification, transaction pattern review and a documented decision on every file.

Result. Structured risk reports and SARs/UARs reviewed by compliance leadership, audit-ready case registers, and recommended fixes for control gaps in AML/CFT procedures.

EDD SAR/UAR Sanctions OSINT
Case 02 · Commercial bank · Diamond Trust Bank Kenya · 2022–2023

Financial crime investigations under CBK supervision

The problem. AML/CFT alerts, sanctions hits and suspicious customer behaviour across retail and commercial portfolios.

What I did. Investigated using follow-the-money analysis, internal records and OSINT; resolved watchlist matches at onboarding and in ongoing monitoring; ran EDD on high-risk accounts.

Result. Investigation memoranda supporting escalation to compliance leadership, and evidence gathered for filing with the regulator.

Investigations Watchlist screening CBK FATF
Case 03 · Agri-fintech · Antugrow · Co-founder · 2023–2025

Onboarding smallholder farmers to on-chain credit

The problem. Productive farmers with no credit history or collateral a lender recognises, and lenders with no reliable way to judge whether a farm can absorb and repay working capital.

What I did. As co-founder and projects and product lead, I mapped the full financial journey of onboarding a farmer, from identity verification and farm records to an assessment grounded in real-world assets, and designed the risk-based onboarding and transaction protocols behind it.

Result. A borrower assessment model that gave both microlenders and mainstream lenders, through on-chain liquidity pools, a verifiable basis for lending to farmers.

Onboarding design KYC Credit risk Real-world assets
Case 04 · Forensic audit · Muita Njoroge Associates · 2021–2022

Tracing income flows in agricultural processing

The problem. Tea factories needed assurance that income was being used as reported and that their ledgers held up.

What I did. Traced income and expenditure through the records, tested general ledger entries against P&L statements, and checked compliance with regulatory requirements.

Result. Control recommendations aimed at cutting preventable losses and tightening process flows.

Forensic audit Ledger testing Controls

How I work a case

Step 1
Scope

Risk-rate the subject and the relationship. Decide what "enough" looks like before starting.

Step 2
Gather

Internal records, transaction history, screening results and open sources.

Step 3
Analyse

Follow the money. Test the customer's story against the evidence. Name the red flags.

Step 4
Decide

Record the rationale, not just the outcome. Someone will read it in two years' time.

Step 5
Act

File the SAR/UAR, enhance monitoring, exit or clear, and close the loop.
